01 / the why

I never fit inside one job title.

I'm not trying to build a company that pretends to know everything. I'm building a place where I can keep learning everything that interests me.

I started with a question. Then another. Then I couldn't stop.

Some people pick a lane. I kept finding side roads: photography, storytelling, local history, code, AI, virtual worlds, and machines with one more secret hiding inside them.

I don't make things just to put a price tag on them. When I can, I release my work openly, because something I build for fun might become the tool someone else needed.
