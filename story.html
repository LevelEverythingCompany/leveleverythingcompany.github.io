The person / not the pitch

I was never just one thing.

This page isn't a résumé. It's an attempt to explain why I keep making things that don't belong in the same category.

I'm Alex. I've spent years chasing whatever sparks my imagination. Photography gave me a way to notice details. Media taught me to tell stories. Technology gave me a way to turn “what if” into something that actually runs.

I like the unusual problems: getting a phone to notice forgotten history nearby, making a tool for people in virtual worlds, or learning what a machine can do that nobody thought to ask it to do.

Some of those ideas become useful apps. Some become experiments. Some fail, and teach me far more than the comfortable ones would have.

I don't want one road. I want the map.

Why the new name?

Wommper1 has been my online identity for a long time. Alex Shade Photography and Alex Shade Productions gave the visual and creative sides of my work their own place. But what I was building kept spilling past those boundaries.

The Level Everything Company isn't a claim that I've mastered everything. It's permission to stay interested in more than one thing. No single skill tree. No arbitrary limit on what I can try next.

Why share the work?

Because curiosity spreads. When I put a project out in the open, somebody can learn from it, challenge it, fix something I missed, or turn it into an idea of their own. I'd rather make things people can use and explore than collect polished screenshots of things I never released.

I still want to make beautiful things. I still want to tell stories. But I also want to build the tools that help those stories travel.

What's next?

More open-source software. More real-world experiments. More creative tools. Probably a few directions I can't explain yet, because I haven't discovered them.

That's the point of Level Everything. It stays unfinished in the best possible way.
