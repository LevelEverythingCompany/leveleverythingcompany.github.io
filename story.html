Alex Shade / the person behind the work

I want you to know how I see the world.

I'm a thinker, inventor, and visionary. I've wanted to be a thinker for as long as I can remember. The Level Everything Company is where I give those thoughts somewhere to go.

I'm Alex Shade, owner of The Level Everything Company, an independent creator brand in Southern Indiana.

You can see an app, a photograph, or a project and know what I made. Understanding me takes a little more. There is a lot of thinking behind those things: questions, possibilities, and connections I want to follow far enough to see what happens.

I want people to know that part of me. The imagination, the effort, and how much I care about turning a thought into something real.

What I mean by inventor and visionary

Inventing starts, for me, with asking whether something could work differently. Could a familiar tool have another use? Could I make something that helps with this problem? What would happen if I brought these ideas together?

Being a visionary means I can imagine a possibility and give it a direction. It also means I have work to do. An idea needs an attempt, an attempt needs testing, and sometimes the result sends me back to the beginning.

I am still learning. I want the work I share to show both the ambition and the limits of where I am.

I don't want one road. I want the map.

Look at what keeps showing up

Southern Indiana Lore: I keep asking what stories are around us that we never notice. Local history and folklore became a project people can explore on their phones or in a browser.

Math Garden: My kids learning math became part of a game with coins, a growing garden, and visible progress. Making something can also mean making a different way to learn.

My family display and calendar projects: I want technology to have a useful place in everyday life, helping us keep track of plans and find things to experience together.

Local AI and my tools: I want to understand what I can do with the equipment I have, adapt it to my needs, and build a way of working that fits how I think.

Photography, scanners, machines, and VR: I keep looking for another possibility inside something familiar. Sometimes that leads to an image. Sometimes it leads to an experiment or a tool.

Those are different kinds of projects, at different stages. Together, they show my interest in helping people notice, learn, create, and connect. Math Garden and my family tools are part of that story; they are not listed here as public releases.

How I use AI

AI is part of how I research, develop ideas, write code, and investigate problems. I bring the questions and choose what I want to build. I use the tools to explore ways of getting there, then keep checking the result against what I actually wanted.

Generated code and a successful build do not answer every question. The public projects include requirements, known limits, and testing evidence because I want people to understand what they are trying.

Why Level Everything?

Wommper1, Alex Shade Photography, and Alex Shade Productions each held a part of my work. My interests kept connecting across them. I wanted one home with room for that.

Level Everything gives me room to grow across those interests and bring what I learn from one into another. Photography can shape how I tell a story. A story can lead me into local history. That history can become a reason to build software.

I want to make a life where thinking, creating, and learning have a real place. This company is something I am building toward that life.
