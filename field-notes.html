Field note 001 / A new beginning

Why this place exists.

I used to think each new interest needed its own name and its own place. One for photos. Another for stories. One for coding. Another for an experiment that started at midnight and got completely out of control.

Now I'd rather have one home where the threads are allowed to cross.

That's what these field notes are for. Not every idea will be a masterpiece. But the process deserves a record: what I tried, what broke, what surprised me, and what someone else might be able to use.

If a question feels too weird to belong here, it's probably exactly the kind of question I want to investigate.

The next entries will show real builds, including the unfinished edges, screenshots, dates, limitations, and links to the work.

Field note 002 / October 24, 2026

The release is only the beginning.

A working app still needs a way for someone else to understand it. This workshop update adds screenshots from the project repositories, direct routes to app releases, and requirements beside each build.

That also means showing the rough edges. DualCast still needs more testing with two physical Bluetooth devices. The translator’s SteamVR and phone experiences need fuller acceptance testing. Lore’s site badges separate sourced history from uncertain claims, and its web atlas is still a beta.

The useful evidence lives with the code: setup instructions, verification notes, known limitations, and issue trackers. Those links now sit beside the download buttons, where they can help someone decide what to try.
